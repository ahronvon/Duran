Moonlight Academy

Written by J. Rivera

CHAPTER 1

The Hidden Classroom

On her first night at Moonlight Academy, Nia follows a trail of silver dust into a classroom missing from every map. Inside, a teacher writes symbols that glow like stars. Nia learns that every student has a gift, but hers is unusual: she can hear memories trapped in objects. Her first lesson comes from a cracked pocket watch, whose memories warn her that someone is trying to erase the school’s history.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, J. looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
