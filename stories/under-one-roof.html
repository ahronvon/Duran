Family

Under One Roof

Written by Mika Torres

Three siblings learn to share responsibility when their parents work away from home.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

Sunday Dinner

With their parents working in another city, the Santos siblings manage school, chores, and their grandmother’s needs. Arguments over dishes and homework become daily events. When they nearly forget an important family celebration, they realize they have been acting like strangers in the same house. They create a simple Sunday dinner tradition where everyone contributes and everyone gets heard.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Mika looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
