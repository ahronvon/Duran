The New Neighbor

Written by Ella Mendoza

A mistaken delivery brings two neighbors together.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

Package 12B

A parcel meant for apartment 12B lands at 12D, where organized Bea lives beside chaotic musician Ren. Their attempts to return the package turn into a series of awkward hallway meetings. When they discover the parcel contains a birthday gift for Ren’s little sister, Bea helps him plan a surprise. Their opposite personalities become less annoying and more interesting with every conversation.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Ella looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
