The Missing Photograph

Written by Caleb Tan

A family photo disappears, revealing a secret hidden for decades.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

The Frame

During a renovation, Sam notices that one photograph is missing from the family wall. His grandmother becomes unusually quiet when he asks about it. A note behind the frame leads him to an old train station and a name nobody in the family mentions. Sam’s search reveals a difficult choice made long ago—and a chance to understand the people he thought he knew.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Caleb looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
