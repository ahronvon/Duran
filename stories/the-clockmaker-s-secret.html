Written by Felix Ramos

CHAPTER 1

One Minute More

At the back of the workshop, Isa finds a brass clock that stops the world for exactly one minute. She uses it to fix mistakes and avoid embarrassment, until she realizes every pause leaves a tiny mark on the clockmaker’s hands. To protect him, she must return the clock to its place and face a problem without borrowing extra time.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Felix looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
