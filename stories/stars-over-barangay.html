Stars Over Barangay

Written by Rhea Castillo

Young volunteers organize a neighborhood stargazing night.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

Looking Up

When a power interruption leaves the barangay in darkness, a science club member brings a telescope outside. Neighbors gather to look at the moon and bright planets. Inspired, the students plan a stargazing night and invite children who have never used a telescope. For one evening, people who rarely talk share stories under the same sky.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Rhea looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
