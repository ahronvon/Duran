The Little Inventor

Written by Gabriel Uy

A student’s inventions solve small problems in very unexpected ways.

CHAPTER 1

The Automatic Alarm

Ben builds an alarm that promises to wake him on time. Instead, it wakes the entire neighborhood, feeds the cat, and launches his slippers across the room. His classmates laugh until the invention accidentally helps them win a school science fair. Ben learns that failure is often just an idea that needs a few more adjustments.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Gabriel looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
