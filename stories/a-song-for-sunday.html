A Song for Sunday

Written by Jae Villanueva

A nervous singer prepares to perform at a neighborhood celebration.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

The First Note

Luna sings beautifully when she is alone, but the thought of an audience makes her voice disappear. Her grandfather teaches her to focus on one friendly face instead of the entire crowd. On Sunday, she steps onto the small stage and dedicates the song to him. Her voice shakes at first, then grows stronger as neighbors join the chorus.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Jae looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
