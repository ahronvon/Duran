Night Shift

Written by Ramon Silva

A security guard notices a pattern in a series of late-night break-ins.

CHAPTER 1

The Blue Light

During his night shift at a shopping center, Arnel notices that the security cameras flicker just before certain doors open. Instead of accusing anyone, he logs every incident and compares the times. His careful notes reveal that the break-ins are staged to hide an employee’s theft. Arnel reports the evidence and learns that patience can be more useful than suspicion.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Ramon looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
