Beyond the Fence

Written by Lia Flores

A family faces change when the home they love is threatened.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

The Notice

The paper taped to their gate is brief: the property will be sold. For the Reyes family, the small house is more than wood and concrete; it holds birthdays, arguments, and the memory of their father. Ana decides to search for a solution while her brother insists they should leave. As neighbors gather to help, the family learns that home is built by people as much as by walls.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Lia looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
