Whispers in the Library

Written by Hannah Chua

A library volunteer hears a voice coming from a book with blank pages.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

The Unwritten Story

The voice begins with one sentence: “You forgot the ending.” Each time Ella opens the blank book, another line appears. She learns that the story belonged to a former librarian who never finished writing it. With help from the library’s oldest records, Ella tries to discover what ending the missing author wanted—and why the book chose her.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Hannah looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
