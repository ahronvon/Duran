The Captain’s Promise

Written by Luis Dela Cruz

A young sailor must guide his crew through a dangerous passage.

CHAPTER 1

Into the Blue

When the captain falls ill during a sudden squall, apprentice sailor Tomas must use everything he has learned to guide the boat toward shelter. The sea is dark, the compass is unreliable, and the crew is frightened. Tomas remembers the captain’s advice: leadership is not about sounding fearless, but helping everyone take the next step. He calls out a plan and asks each crew member to do one task.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Luis looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
