Hope

When the Rain Stops

Written by Mae Dizon

After a difficult year, a student finds a fresh start through a small promise.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

Clear Skies

The school year has been full of setbacks, and Bea is tired of pretending everything is fine. One afternoon, her teacher asks the class to write one promise they can realistically keep. Bea writes, “I will give myself another chance.” She starts with manageable goals, reconnects with a friend, and learns that a fresh start is rarely dramatic; often, it is simply the next morning.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Mae looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
