CHAPTER 1

The Unseen File

Kai is finishing a simple attendance app when a file appears that he never created. It contains names, dates, and a warning: “Do not open the old server.” Curiosity wins. With his best friend Bea, he investigates the abandoned computer lab and discovers that the file is connected to a student who disappeared years ago. Every answer creates a new question, and someone seems to know they are looking.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, Ethan looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
