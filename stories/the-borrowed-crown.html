The Borrowed Crown

Written by K. Bautista

A village baker is mistaken for a missing royal heir.

Reading progress0%

CHAPTER 1

Royal for a Day

Mila delivers bread to the palace on the same morning the royal heir vanishes. Because of a family resemblance, the guards mistake her for the princess and ask her to attend a public ceremony. Mila could run away, but she notices the villagers need help with a water shortage. Her unexpected honesty earns the respect of the court and helps uncover where the real heir went.

As the day moved forward, the lesson became clearer: life rarely gives us a perfect map. It gives us choices, people who walk beside us, and chances to try again. The smallest decision can become the beginning of something meaningful.

When the moment finally came to move on, K. looked ahead with a little more confidence. The story was not finished, but this chapter had taught one important truth: every new beginning starts with the courage to take the first step.
